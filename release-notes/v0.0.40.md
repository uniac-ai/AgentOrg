0.0.40 is on npm under the `next` tag, as 0.0.39 was: `npm install -g @agentorg/cli@next`. `latest` stays 0.0.15. It pins AgentComputer 0.0.41, as 0.0.39 did.

## What you meet

- **A rebuilt page.**
  - **The list beside cards.** The bar of icons is gone. The list lies on the page's ground at the left, and what you open is in cards beside it: a conversation on Chat, an agent on Team. Search is at the top of the list's side, and Chat, Team and Settings are along its foot.
  - **The list is yours to size.** Drag the line beside it to set its width; a double click puts it back. Put the list away from the button at its top, and the card takes the window.
  - **An agent's face.** Each agent has a face: the one picked when it was made, or else one its id gives it. Lists show it still. In a conversation's head, its details and its page, it is alive: it reads while the agent works and sleeps while it is stopped.
  - **A conversation's head** is the agent's face in a round frame, over a pill that says how it stands.
  - **Pinned conversations.** Pin a conversation from its row's pin button, its "⋯" menu or a right-click, and it becomes a tile above the list. Drag a tile to another place, a row onto the tiles to pin it, or a tile off them to unpin it.
  - **Delete chat**, in the same menu, takes a conversation off the list. Nothing said in it is cleared, and the agent keeps working.
  - **A row says when** its last message was said: the time today, the date before.
  - **The conversation itself.**
    - One side's bubbles in a row read as one group.
    - The agent's reaction is an emoji in a round chip.
    - A message's menu says when it was sent.
    - The room stays where you scrolled it.
  - **An agent's details and its computer** open at the right of the conversation's card, from its face and from the screen button, where the page has room. Elsewhere, the face opens the agent's page on Team.
  - **The chat box** is one line with a plus for a picture and Send, and grows only as your words need.
  - **An agent is made on one card.** Pick its face from a row that never ends, each face with a name it offers. Type its name and what it's for in place, and Create. The template is a badge on the card, and what it gives opens from the card's edge.
- **Memory on offer.**
  - **A fresh home's first Assistant is made with memory.** It remembers what you ask it to keep, from its first conversation.
  - **Memory can be added to any agent from its page:** Team, the agent, Setup, Add a unit. It is listed until the agent holds it. A template can name it too, from Settings.
  - **An Assistant a home already has** gets no memory by itself. Add it from its page, or ask an Assistant with control to add it to itself, as in 0.0.39.
- **`agentorg uninstall` also removes the Mac app**, where the install line put it, in one command.
  - It also removes the app's privacy grants and what the app keeps in your Library.
  - The app goes first, and the command says it can take up to 45 seconds. If its removal fails, nothing more is removed past the service, so running the command again tries again.
  - Where AgentOrg came from npm, a Mac app there stays, and the command to remove it is printed, as before.
  - macOS keeps a switched-off "AgentOrg" in Login Items, which no app can remove; the last line says so.

## Built, and not yet met

- **`agents@2`, beside `agents@1`.** Management serves it: it makes an agent empty, and mounts and unmounts what the agent is made of. The page still reads `agents@1`.
- **Templates as capabilities.** On `agents@2`, a template is a list of capabilities, mounted together. The app ships `assistant`: the computer, the web, memory and control.
- **The computer, the web and MCP servers as capabilities.** Each one's unit form is its arguments. An agent may hold an MCP server more than once, one for each server.
- **Control's arguments** are its unit's form. With no templates named, control still mounts, as in 0.0.39, with nothing to create agents from.
- **Control an agent gives an agent it made** is held to its own: nothing beyond what it holds, within what the other agent's computer reaches, and one level less deep. Where its agents can't make agents, it gives none.
- **How a mount from outside stands.** Management's `get` asks each provider outside the agent how that agent's mount stands. A mount its provider no longer has is failed; one whose provider can't be reached is down. Only `get` asks: nothing on an agent's turn does.
- **A capability added to a running agent keeps what it provides to that agent.** A service it brings, which the agent didn't know when it started, is seen by that agent's other capabilities and by no other agent.

## What you need to do

- **A home from 0.0.38 or earlier:** as when 0.0.39 opened such a home.
- **Going back to 0.0.39** keeps your agents, memory included: checked on one home, made on this release, opened by 0.0.39 and by this release again. What it loses:
  - **An agent's picked face.** 0.0.39 leaves it out, without a word, the first time it saves its agents. Back on 0.0.40, the agent's face is the one its id gives it.
  - **By the same rule, from the code:** templates of capabilities saved through `agents@2`, and the record of what a mount from outside an agent made. The page reaches neither.
  - **On 0.0.39's page,** conversations taken off the list on 0.0.40 are listed again, and nothing is pinned.
- **The install line** still installs nothing while no signed Mac app is published.
