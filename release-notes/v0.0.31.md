0.0.31 is on npm under the `next` tag, as 0.0.30 was: `npm install -g @agentorg/cli@next`. `latest` stays 0.0.15. It uses AgentComputer 0.0.39 and its SDK.

## What 0.0.31 holds, since 0.0.30: #315, #316, #318, #320, #325, #353, #358, #359 and the pin of AgentComputer 0.0.39, with #352 and #355 for development

### What you meet

**Every agent works and speaks to one set of words** (#315, #316, #318, #320, #325)

- **Every agent is told one frame** for how it works and speaks, the parts that fit its role:
  - the judgment it works by;
  - that replies are short and plain;
  - how a message is weighed, by who sent it, and what your yes covers;
  - that a step taken in your name waits for your own yes.
- **What that showed in the measured runs:**
  - The front desk, which reads your message first, now answers a short question of fact itself, such as "What's the capital of Australia?", rather than starting work for it.
  - "Delete everything in your workspace" is asked about first, where before it was done.
- **A worker writes its replies as the page splits them:** one thought per bubble, a list kept with the line above it, and, in a longer message, the first line alone.
- **A reply that only sends ends the work's turn,** with no last request after it, so "Working" goes sooner once the answer is whole. So a progress line sent alone also ends it; the agent is told to make its next call beside such a line to keep working.
- **When a piece of work's tools change** between its turns, it is told so once, at its next turn, and its Activity shows a line naming them: "Tools changed. New: … Gone: …". A change across a restart of the app isn't told.
- **The Assistant template now says only who the agent is.** How it works is the frame's. An agent made from it before keeps its stored words. Where those are the template's four lines as they were, it is told the new line in their place; words it was given after them, or edited ones, are its own and stay.
- **The computer's own words** come to the agent while its skills are given, and its skills no longer repeat what the frame says. Two lines went with them:
  - the web search skill's note that searches go to an outside service;
  - the password skills' exception for a password you gave it. The frame says never to type passwords.
- **Measured, with a set of 19 conversations, two runs before these and two after,** before #358 and the pin:
  - the conversations meeting their checks went from 16 and 15 to 18 and 18. In each run, 7 or 8 of them were judged by reading the replies, not by an automatic check;
  - model requests went from 99 and 97 to 77 and 71, while input tokens stayed about the same;
  - the words in all replies went from about 450 to about 320;
  - a run took about 5 minutes 20 seconds, against 8.

  One conversation, an ambiguous "Clean it up.", got no reply in one of the two runs after, and was answered in the other.
- **Why,** in the words behind it: "Right now its not very usable. The response is slow, response is long, nothing message like".

**A message the agent answered first stays answered** (#358)

- When the agent's front desk answered a message itself, such as a greeting, and then started work in the same conversation, the work's end showed "Not answered" under the greeting. It no longer does.
- A conversation from before 0.0.31 whose greeting was answered then can still show it once, if work takes that conversation later.

**A unit's card in Setup folds, and is drawn whole on a phone** (#353)

- In Setup's Change… and a way forward's window, the card has a Fold on its head: "Fold Computer", or "Open Computer" once folded. Folded, it is its head alone, and what was typed stays.
- The card is no longer cut at 60% of the screen with a scroll of its own, also on a phone. The window scrolls as a whole only where it is taller than the screen.
- Change… opens with no field focused.

### What changes nothing you see

- **AgentComputer 0.0.39** is pinned, the program and its SDK. Its own changes are in its Mac app: turning a part on asks macOS for nothing, Screen Recording's Set up works as Accessibility's, and its `uninstall` waits up to 45 seconds for its provider to end. No Mac app of 0.0.39 is published yet, so no one installing 0.0.31 meets them.
- **The release run makes its own GitHub release** (#359), once npm has the version, not marked latest:
  - the core and the frontend, each alone;
  - `install.sh`;
  - the Mac app, once one is pinned;
  - `SHA256SUMS` over them;
  - each attested, with these notes.

  0.0.31 is the first release made this way, and the first to carry `install.sh`.
- **Nothing stored changes, and no contract.**
- **The release's Mac app job** carries nothing while `mac-app.json` pins no sum, as before. The install line still installs nothing.

## What you need to do

- **A home from 0.0.30:** nothing to do. Going back to 0.0.30 hasn't been checked.
- **A home from 0.0.29 or earlier:** as when 0.0.30 opened such a home.
- **An agent you made from the Assistant template** before 0.0.31 keeps its stored words, and is told the new ones in their place. Words of your own after them are kept as they are.
- The install line now names 0.0.31, and still installs nothing while no Mac app is published.

## Not run on a Mac

- The measured runs used ChatGPT plan usage, with each agent's computer a container, not a Mac.
- The unit card was checked in a headless browser, by the page's tests and by measuring it against the design.
- As in 0.0.30, these were checked with stand-ins on a scratch account, or in tests:
  - the service under the real launchd, across a login and a restart;
  - the install line, `agentorg update` and `agentorg uninstall`;
  - the first Assistant made with the real AgentOrg app;
  - a permission taken away and given back;
  - using your screen and your own browser through AgentOrg.
