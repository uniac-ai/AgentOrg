## What you meet

- **The licences of what the page bundles come with it,** in the cli and in the frontend download, which also carries AgentOrg's own licence. The install scripts state theirs, MIT.

### With AgentComputer 0.0.41

AgentComputer 0.0.41 is pinned, its SDK and its Mac app's version. Its changes are in its program, so you meet them where your computer runs AgentComputer 0.0.41: install it with `npm install -g @agentorg/computer@next`, then stop the one running with `agentcomputer down`, and AgentOrg starts it again. No Mac app of 0.0.41 is published yet.

- **"In use" shows an agent while its call runs,** and for 10 seconds after the call ends. Before, it showed the agent only for a call's first 10 seconds, or, for a browser call, only once it had answered. A pause of more than 10 seconds between two calls still shows nobody.
- **A container computer no longer has ffmpeg** once it runs on AgentComputer 0.0.41.
