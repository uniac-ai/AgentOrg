## What you meet

- **Typing dots while the agent works on your message.** Three dots show at the end of the conversation, on the agent's side, in place of the line that sat under each of your messages. They go when the agent replies, when it decides your message needs no reply, when it stops, or when something goes wrong.
- **A thank-you is closed quietly.** The agent is told that a message that asks for nothing, like thanks, needs no reply: it closes it, the dots go, and the message isn't marked as not answered.
- **Chats lists your conversations only.** An agent shows there once you have written to it, and the others are in Team. Until you have a conversation, the first agent's is open and ready for your first message.
- **"You're offline."** When AgentOrg can't reach its service, a notice at the top says so, and the page under it is blurred and can't be used until the service is back. Press the notice to try again at once.
- **Every agent's face is a rounded square,** wherever it shows.
- **A selected row keeps its colour** when you point at it, in Chats, Team and the bar on the left.
- **No tooltip is left open after a window or menu closes,** as one was after closing Settings.
- **The install line and `agentorg update`** take the installer from the release they install, and check it against that release's checksums before running it. In the few minutes between a version reaching npm and its release going up, they say so and install nothing.
