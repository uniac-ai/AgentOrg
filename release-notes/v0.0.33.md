0.0.33 is on npm under the `next` tag, as 0.0.32 was: `npm install -g @agentorg/cli@next`. `latest` stays 0.0.15. It uses AgentComputer 0.0.40, which changes nothing that runs since 0.0.39, only how AgentComputer is released.

## What you meet

- **A menu on the agent's bubbles.** Point at a bubble of words and press "⋯" for Copy, which copies the whole message, with how long ago it was sent; on a phone, hold the bubble still for half a second. The Copy under each of the agent's messages is gone, and a code block keeps its own Copy and Download.
- **Messages sit closer:** 4 px apart from one side, and 16 px where the side changes.
- **Chat's list has a compose icon** at the end of its head. It opens a "To" picker of all your agents: type to narrow it, and pick one to open its conversation, or an empty one if you haven't written to it yet. On a phone, it leads from "No chats" to a conversation without going through Team.
- **Search,** above Chat on the bar on the left, or first along the foot on a phone. It finds your conversations by their agent's name or the line Chats shows for them, and your agents by name. ⌘K opens it on a Mac, iPhone or iPad, and Ctrl K elsewhere, except in a terminal, which keeps Ctrl K.
- **On a phone held upright, every field's words are 16 px,** so an iPhone no longer zooms the page when you type.
