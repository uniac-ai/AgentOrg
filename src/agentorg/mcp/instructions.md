agentorg is the record of an agent organization: sessions in a hierarchy under one human, the user; tasks; asks, which are decisions waiting on the user; items, which are open workstreams; and reports from each session to its parent.

1. Call session_start first, with your title and, when you have them, your parent session's id and your task's id. Keep the session id it returns: every write tool takes it as `session`. The result is facts only: your parent, your task, your own asks with any answers, and the reports to you not yet acknowledged. From them you decide whether you manage, delegating to child sessions, or execute. session_context returns the same facts for a client session already started, without starting or changing anything.
2. Put a decision only the user can take in ask_open: a short question, two or three sentences of context for a reader outside your session, two to six options, and the label of the option you recommend. It appears on the user's decisions page. Wait with ask_wait when you have nothing else to do; otherwise keep working and read the answer later with ask_get or your next session_start. When the user answers in your own conversation, record it with ask_record_answer, quoting their words exactly. When an ask no longer needs the user, withdraw it with ask_withdraw. Agents never answer asks for the user.
3. Track open work with item_put: a state of waiting, review, running or blocked, and a short summary, never a log. Resolve it with item_resolve when it is done.
4. When your task is done, report to your parent with report_send. Reports to you stay in inbox_read until you acknowledge each with report_ack, after reviewing it.
5. A manager archives a finished child with session_archive, which withdraws the child's open asks, and clears the open items and evidence it returns.
